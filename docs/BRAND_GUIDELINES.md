# LUMAD LINGUA - Brand Identity & UI/UX Design Guidelines
**Version 1.0 | Flutter Mobile Application**

---

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Brand Name
**Lumad Lingua**

### Brand Category
Educational Technology — Gamified Indigenous Language Learning

### Primary Language
Mansaka

### Brand Tagline
**Learn the language. Keep the stories alive.**

### Brand Mission
To make Mansaka language learning accessible, interactive, and enjoyable through gamified educational experiences, authentic language resources, and modern technology.

### Brand Personality
- **Friendly:** Welcoming to beginners and learners of all ages.
- **Playful:** Encourages exploration through enjoyable activities.
- **Encouraging:** Celebrates achievements and motivates continued learning.
- **Authentic:** Respects the language and cultural heritage represented.
- **Modern:** Combines innovative technology with meaningful educational experiences.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Primary Display Font**: **Baloo 2** (SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold) for splash branding, unit titles, rewards, and major headings.
- **Secondary Body Font**: **Nunito Sans** (Regular, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold) for body copy, quiz options, buttons, and dictionary text.

### Scale
- **Display**: 34 sp (Baloo 2 ExtraBold)
- **Heading 1**: 28 sp (Baloo 2 Bold)
- **Heading 2**: 24 sp (Baloo 2 Bold)
- **Heading 3**: 20 sp (Nunito Sans ExtraBold)
- **Body Large**: 18 sp (Nunito Sans Regular)
- **Body**: 16 sp (Nunito Sans Regular)
- **Button**: 16 sp (Nunito Sans ExtraBold)
- **Caption**: 13 sp (Nunito Sans SemiBold)
- **Small Label**: 12 sp (Nunito Sans Bold)
